# Global Superstore Sales - Data Visualization

## Overview
This project transforms the Global Superstore Sales dataset into compelling visual stories as part of the CodeAlpha Data Analytics Internship.

## Dataset
- **Source:** Kaggle - Global Superstore Dataset
- **Records:** 9,994 sales transactions
- **Features:** 21 columns including sales, profit, region, category, segment and discount

## Visualizations Created
1. Total Sales by Region - Bar Chart
2. Total Profit by Product Category - Bar Chart
3. Sales Trend Over the Years - Line Chart
4. Sales vs Profit Relationship - Scatter Plot
5. Top 10 Products by Total Sales - Horizontal Bar Chart
6. Sales Share by Customer Segment - Pie Chart
7. Profit Distribution by Region - Box Plot
8. Impact of Discount on Profit - Scatter Plot

## Key Insights
- The West region generates the highest total sales
- Technology is the most profitable product category
- Sales have grown consistently year over year
- Higher discounts consistently lead to lower profits
- The Consumer segment accounts for the largest share of sales
